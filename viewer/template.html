Model notes

Finite volume, MUSCL–minmod reconstruction, hydrostatic reconstruction for a well-balanced bed term, Rusanov flux, SSP-RK2. Basal Coulomb friction is applied as an implicit stopping step, so material halts where the slope falls below φbed. Not yet ported from Titan2D: adaptive mesh refinement, bed-curvature (centripetal) term, internal-friction shear term, and the two-phase Pitman–Le model.
